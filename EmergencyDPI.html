01 • THE PROBLEM

In an emergency, information can disappear.

When a patient cannot speak, doctors may need a few critical facts immediately — without opening an entire medical record.

⏱

Time critical

Emergency decisions can depend on information that is not immediately available.

🔒

Privacy sensitive

A complete medical record can reveal far more information than an emergency team needs.

📍

Unfamiliar hospital

Patients may arrive at a provider that has no existing relationship with them.

Emergency care needs the right information — not the entire medical record.
02 • THE GAP

EmergencyDPI is not another medical-record platform. It is an emergency-focused access layer built around minimum necessary data, consent, time-limited access and auditability.

04 • BUILT-IN CONTROLS

Privacy is part of the workflow.

🔐

Consent-controlled access

Patient or authorized person controls sharing wherever the emergency protocol permits.

🎯

Minimum necessary data

Providers request specific emergency fields instead of receiving an unrestricted record.

⏱

Time-limited access

Approved access has a defined duration and can expire automatically.

✓

Verified requester

Hospital/provider identity is checked before information is released.

📋

Portable profile
